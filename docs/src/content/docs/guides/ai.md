Beat Scrobble can use Large Language Models (LLMs) to analyze your listening habits, generate critiques, and provide insights into your musical teste.

## Configuration

We use **OpenRouter** as a unified gateway to access top-tier models like GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Llama 3.

1.  Get an API Key from [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/).
2.  In Beat Scrobble, go to **Admin > Settings > Services**.
3.  Enter your **OpenRouter API Key**.
4.  (Optional) Select your preferred model. We recommend `anthropic/claude-3.5-sonnet` for the best balance of quality and speed.

## Features

### "Roast My Taste"
The AI "Critique" feature will analyze your top artists and tracks to give you a personalized (and often savage) review of your music taste.

(Coming Soon) The AI will provide daily summaries of your mood and listening patterns.
